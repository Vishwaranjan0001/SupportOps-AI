REFUND POLICY — Fictional SupportOps Store
A customer may request a refund within 7 calendar days after delivery.
The order must be marked eligible in our database before a refund request is submitted.
Damaged items require a photograph for review. The support agent may request evidence.
Creating a refund request does not issue money. A human manager must approve or reject it.
This demo only records approval status; it never makes a payment or transfers funds.
